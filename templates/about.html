Digital Food Waste Reduction and Awareness System for Households

Project Topic: Food Waste Reduction in Households • Purpose: To study household food-waste practices and provide a digital solution for reducing avoidable food waste.

Course: B.Sc. Computer Science
Project Type: Community Engagement (CEP)
Methodology: Survey & Software Intervention
Problem Statement

Household food waste accounts for a significant portion of municipal organic waste. Due to fast-paced lifestyles, lack of inventory visibility, uncoordinated grocery purchasing, and poor leftover tracking, families frequently discard edible food. This results in unnecessary household financial loss and generates landfill greenhouse gas emissions.

Project Objectives
  • Analyze food-waste habits across a target group of 10–20 real households using Google Forms.
  • Develop a user-friendly digital application for active food inventory, expiry tracking, meal planning, and leftover logging.
  • Raise community awareness regarding proper food storage, FIFO principles, and responsible consumption.
  • Quantify waste discards and provide dynamic analytical feedback to users.
Research Methodology
  1. Field Survey: Conduct an anonymous, structured 17-question survey with 10–20 households via Google Forms.
  2. Data Entry & Aggregation: Securely enter real responses into SQLite and compute live statistics and dynamic interpretations.
  3. Software Development: Build an intuitive Flask web application adhering to strict data integrity standards.
  4. Community Engagement: Share awareness guides and demonstrate software utility to participants.
Expected Outcomes
  • Greater awareness of expiry timelines and inventory rotation in participating homes.
  • Practical reduction in preventable grocery discards and financial expenditure.
  • Empirically backed insights regarding primary drivers of domestic food waste.
Study Limitations
  • Sample Size: The survey is conducted with an initial sample of 10–20 households, representing local exploratory findings rather than universal demographic patterns.
  • Self-Reported Estimates: Weight and monetary values are based on user estimations.
  • No Food Safety Guarantees: Leftover and expiry notifications serve as informational trackers and do not replace visual/sensory hygiene evaluations.
Future Scope
  • Barcode / Receipt Scanning: Integrating OCR and barcode scanning for automated receipt-to-inventory entry.
  • Push / SMS Notifications: Automated WhatsApp or email notifications for items expiring within 24 hours.
  • Community Food Sharing: Neighborhood peer-to-peer portal for surplus sealed food donation.
  • AI Recipe Generation: Dynamic recipe suggestions based exclusively on available expiring ingredients.
