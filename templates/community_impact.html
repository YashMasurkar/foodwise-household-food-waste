1
Problem Identified in the Community

In urban and suburban households, substantial quantities of edible food are routinely discarded due to lack of systematic inventory visibility, forgotten expiry dates in refrigerators, over-preparation of cooked dishes, and inadequate meal scheduling.

Economic Leakage

Households spend valuable income on groceries that are never eaten, directly increasing living costs.

Landfill Methane

Organic waste in anaerobic municipal landfills emits greenhouse gases, exacerbating local environmental degradation.

Lack of Tools

Most households rely purely on memory rather than digital tracking tools for kitchen management.

2
Community Engagement Activity & Protocol

As part of the B.Sc. Computer Science Community Engagement Project (CEP), student researchers actively engaged with approximately 10–20 local households. The engagement encompassed:

  • Initial Community Interaction: Explaining the purpose of the study to household members and distributing an anonymous Google Form questionnaire.
  • Awareness Sharing: Disseminating practical guidelines on FIFO storage, refrigerator organization, and meal portioning.
  • Digital Tool Demonstration: Demonstrating how a web-based inventory and expiry notification system helps prevent spoilage.
3
Survey Methodology & Ethical Standards
Data Collection Protocol
  • Official Instrument: Google Forms (17 standardized questions across 6 sections).
  • Target Sample: 10–20 real households within the target community.
  • Data Entry: Team members enter or batch-import verified collected responses into this web application.
Research Ethics & Integrity
  • Anonymity: No personally identifiable information (names, phone numbers, exact residential addresses) is collected.
  • Zero Fake Data: Strict adherence to authentic responses; no synthetic records are seeded in final evaluations.

The Digital Intervention Modules
Inventory & Expiry Tracker

Maintains item visibility and highlights products needing consumption within 1–3 days.

Weekly Meal Planner

Connects recipes directly with items nearing expiration to prioritize existing stock.

Leftovers & Waste Logger

Tracks prepared leftovers and logs discarded items to quantify financial leakage.
